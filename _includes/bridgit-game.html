Rules

Take turns selecting a line, using one of its remaining tickets. This selects a line rather than a particular edge. Join wins with a circuit through e₁₄; Cut wins with a cocircuit through e₁₄. Wins are checked automatically.

The small graphs show every edge set with the selected player’s ticket counts. They do not assign specific edges to either player. A single possible s–t path need not be a win.
