How to play

Two people share this screen. On your turn, buy one available ticket. There is one ticket per playable edge, shared between both players. In Individual edges mode every edge has its own ticket, giving the ordinary graph game. In Paper example mode the two green edges share a line, as do the two red edges. Each black edge is a separate line.

Join wins with a circuit through the reserved connection; Cut wins with a cocircuit through it. The game checks these conditions automatically and stops as soon as someone wins. Every completed game has exactly one winner.

In the grouped game, finding just one possible route is not enough. For Join, a winning selection of tickets has a forest representation, but every representation either connects s to t or contains a cycle. You may use a smaller selection from your hand to win. Extra tickets never hurt.

Cut’s corresponding winning selection has a representation whose removal leaves the network connected, but removing any representation together with the reserved edge disconnects it.
